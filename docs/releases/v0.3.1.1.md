# Nomad Launcher 0.3.1.1 — Battery fixes

Tap the battery indicator to see measured net power in watts and an estimate of
how long charging or discharging will take.

- Live battery power with **W in / W out** direction, including discharge while plugged in.
- Estimated **time to full** while charging and **time remaining** while discharging.
- Charge gained or lost and average percentage change over the observation period.
- Explicit full, idle and unsupported-sensor states.
- Includes 0.3.1 per-game playtime totals and last-session tracking. Enable Android
  usage access in Settings → Playtime; then launch games through Nomad.

Watts describe power entering/leaving the battery, not the charger’s advertised
rating or wall-socket draw. Estimates use Android's charging estimate, observed
percentage changes or battery-gauge average current, depending on availability.
They vary with game load, screen brightness, temperature and charging taper.
No root is needed for battery readings or playtime tracking.

## Install

Download **nomad-launcher.apk** below and install it over your existing Nomad.
The package and signing identity are unchanged, so your library and settings remain.
Requires Android 8.0 or later. SHA256SUMS and signing certificate details are attached.
